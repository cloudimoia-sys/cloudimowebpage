3. Source of the data

All data comes directly from you. We do not obtain personal data from third parties or from publicly accessible sources.

4. Recipients and processors

We do not disclose your data to third parties except where legally required. We do use service providers acting as data processors, under the contracts required by Article 28 GDPR:

  • Cloudflare, Inc. — website hosting and delivery (Cloudflare Pages). Certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and covered by standard contractual clauses.
  • Formspree, Inc. — processing and forwarding of contact form messages. Data is transmitted to servers in the United States under standard contractual clauses.
  • Google LLC — email service where enquiries are received and stored.

These international transfers are carried out with the safeguards provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR. If you would rather not use the form, you can email us directly at cloudimo.ia@gmail.com or call us.

5. Cookies and tracking technologies

This website uses no first-party or third-party cookies, includes no analytics or advertising tracking pixels, and stores no information in your browser.

The hosting provider records technical connection data (IP address, browser, date and time) solely for security, abuse prevention and service operation, on the basis of the controller's legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Your rights

You may exercise the following rights at any time:

  • Access — find out what data of yours we process.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data when it is no longer necessary.
  • Restriction — ask us to suspend processing in certain circumstances.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Withdrawal of consent — at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise them, write to cloudimo.ia@gmail.com stating the right you wish to exercise. We will respond within one month at most.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the regulations, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (www.aepd.es).

7. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration: encryption in transit via HTTPS, access control, strong authentication on the services involved, and periodic configuration review.

We may update this policy to reflect regulatory changes or new services. The version in force is always the one published on this page, together with its last-updated date.
