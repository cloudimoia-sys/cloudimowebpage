How we work

Four phases, no surprises along the way

Every phase ends with a deliverable and a decision that's yours. You can stop at any point.

01

Assessment

A free first meeting and a review of what you already run: systems, processes, pain points and risks. You walk away with a clear picture of where you stand, whether you hire us or not.

02

Proposal & design

Fixed scope, proposed architecture, timeline and price. No small print, no open-ended hours. You know exactly what will be built and what it will cost.

03

Implementation

Delivered in short increments with regular demos and visible progress. Migrations planned outside critical hours, with a rollback path always ready.

04

Evolution & support

Documentation, knowledge transfer and — if you want it — ongoing maintenance with monitoring and improvements. The goal isn't for you to depend on us; it's for the system to hold.

About Cloudimo

Small by choice, not by size of project

Cloudimo started from a simple idea: most companies don't need a large consultancy that assigns them an account manager and three layers of intermediaries. They need someone who understands their business, can actually build, and answers the phone.

We work with a limited number of clients at a time. That means the person who designs your architecture is the one who implements and maintains it, and the context of your project never gets lost in an internal handover.

Our technical commitment is deliberately boring: solutions that can be understood, documented and maintained. No heroic architectures that only work while their author is in the room.
